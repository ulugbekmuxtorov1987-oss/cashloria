#  Cashloria

Build a professional educational web app called Finly.



Finly is an interactive platform that teaches people how to understand, manage and improve their personal finances.



The learning experience should be inspired by the best language-learning apps: short lessons, levels, progress tracking, quizzes, streaks, XP, achievements and gradual difficulty.



The goal is to make financial education feel simple, addictive, practical and understandable even for someone who knows almost nothing about money.



IMPORTANT CONTENT RULE



Do NOT copy or reproduce entire books, paid courses, copyrighted articles or copyrighted text.



Research financial education using reliable and legally accessible sources on the internet, such as:



- government financial education resources

- central banks

- universities

- reputable financial education organizations

- public-domain materials

- official educational resources

- reputable books and articles only for general concepts



Use these sources to understand concepts and create ORIGINAL lessons in your own words.



Every lesson should include a small "Sources" section with links to the sources used.



Do not pretend that Finly's content is professional financial advice.



Add a disclaimer that the platform provides educational information, not personalized financial, investment, tax or legal advice.

2. LESSON STRUCTURE



Every lesson should be short and interactive.



A lesson should contain:



1. Simple explanation

2. Real-life example

3. Interactive question

4. Multiple-choice quiz

5. Mini challenge

6. Short recap

7. XP reward



Example:



Lesson:



What is a budget?



Explain it using simple language.



Then:



"Imagine you earn $500 per month."



Ask:



"You spend $300 on necessary expenses. How much remains?"



Give four answers.



After the answer, explain WHY it is correct.



Do not simply say "Correct".



Teach the concept.

6. FINANCIAL SIMULATIONS



This should be one of the strongest features of Finly.



Create interactive simulations.



Budget Simulator

Example:

Then choose how to allocate money:



Housing

Food

Transport

Entertainment

Savings

Emergency Fund



The system calculates the remaining money.



Then show the consequences of their choices.



---



Compound Interest Simulator



Allow users to enter:



Initial amount

Monthly contribution

Interest rate

Years



Show an interactive graph.



Explain that returns are not guaranteed.



---



Debt Simulator



Show how different repayment amounts affect the fictional debt over time.



Explain interest clearly.



---



Emergency Fund Simulator



Ask about fictional monthly expenses and calculate a suggested educational target such as 3–6 months of expenses.



Clearly label this as general educational guidance.

Store basic progress locally.

21. FREE-FIRST MODEL



All basic financial education should remain free.



Design the architecture so premium features could potentially be added later, but do not add payments now.



Possible future premium features:



- advanced simulations

- personalized learning paths

- advanced financial planning tools

- additional courses

- offline lessons



Do not lock the core educational content behind a paywall.

22. SAFETY



Never tell users:



"Buy this stock."



"Invest in this cryptocurrency."



"This investment will definitely make money."



"This is guaranteed."



Instead teach concepts such as:



"What is diversification?"



"What is investment risk?"



"What is compound interest?"



"What factors should someone consider before investing?"



Use fictional examples when teaching investing.
